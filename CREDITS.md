# Credits

ProjectTM is a collaborative fork. It would not exist without the following projects and contributors:

- **[Theft4-NX](https://github.com/gabryboy12-ai/Theft4-NX)** — direct Nintendo Switch runtime and platform base.
- **[Theft4](https://github.com/KoreanSeats1/Theft4)** — GTA IV static recompilation and ARM64/iOS research.
- **[Theft4-Android](https://github.com/deivid22srk/Theft4-Android)** and **[LibertyRecompAndroid](https://github.com/vaduur/LibertyRecompAndroid)** — ARM64 runtime, startup, audio, and transition references.
- **[LibertyRecomp](https://github.com/OZORDI/LibertyRecomp)** — GTA IV recompilation infrastructure and runtime foundations.
- **[ReXGlue SDK](https://github.com/rexglue/rexglue-sdk)** — Xbox 360 compatibility runtime, kernel services, memory model, threading, filesystem, audio, and graphics interfaces.
- **[GTA-IV-RECOMP-XBOX-360](https://github.com/luisxl15/GTA-IV-RECOMP-XBOX-360)** — PC recompilation behavior reference used for comparison and debugging.
- **Xenia and XenonRecomp** — foundational Xbox 360 emulation, translation, and research work.
- **[nfsmw-nx](https://github.com/StevensND/nfsmw-nx)** — Nintendo Switch threading, timing, renderer, and platform reference material.
- **[project8-sw](https://github.com/R-drg/project8-sw)** — Switch asynchronous I/O and platform reference material.
- **devkitPro and libnx** — Nintendo Switch homebrew toolchain and system interfaces.
- **Mesa/NVK, Vulkan, SPIR-V, and related open-source projects** — graphics and shader infrastructure.
